# Contributing

Data practices and vendor policies change constantly, so corrections and updates are genuinely valuable here.

## What's welcome

- **Corrections and updates** — a changed default, a new opt-out location, an out-of-date claim.
- **Red-flag additions** — a data practice worth watching that isn't yet listed. Open an issue with a source.
- **Plain-English improvements** — this playbook lives or dies on being understandable to non-experts.
- **Translations** — faithful, credited.

## What isn't

- Legal advice, or anything phrased as a compliance guarantee — this is practitioner guidance.
- Vendor or affiliate placements, or "our tool is safe" additions.
- Scaremongering. The tone is calm and specific, not fearful.

## Standards

Plain, specific, UK English. Claims should be checkable and, where possible, mirror what the [AI Data-Practices Checker](https://github.com/VictorOsondu/ai-data-practices-checker) actually inspects. Open an issue before large changes.
